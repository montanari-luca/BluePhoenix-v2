# Settings

Open **Settings** from the top bar.

## Appearance

- **Theme:** System, Light or Dark.
- **Accent colour:** five colour pairs for buttons and highlights.

## Editors & terminals

Your default editor and terminal, and where the **Run** buttons start commands (embedded or system terminal).

## AI

Local or cloud provider, model, API key. See [AI features](ai.md).

## Command line

Install, reinstall or remove the `bluephoenix` terminal command. See [Command line](cli.md).

## Cloud sync

Connect your own database to keep projects, to-dos and time in sync across your computers and iPhone. See [Cloud sync and the iPhone app](sync.md).

## Backup

BluePhoenix saves a copy of your data every day and keeps the last 7. **Back up now** takes one immediately; **Show folder** opens where they are.

- **Export…** saves everything (projects, to-dos, notes, time, commands, automations, fork settings and preferences) as a `.json` file, or as a `.db` copy of the database. API keys and the database address stay in your keychain and are never exported.
- **Import…** (or **Restore…** next to an automatic backup) merges a backup into what you have: missing items are added and older ones are updated, but **nothing newer than the backup is overwritten**. Imported items sync to your other devices.
